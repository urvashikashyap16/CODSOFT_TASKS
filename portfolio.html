About

Curious about how things work,
stubborn about making them work well.

I'd rather ask a question early than guess for three hours.

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at Graphic Era Hill University, Dehradun, and I write code on both sides of the API. On the frontend I care about interfaces that stay readable on a small screen; on the backend I care about routes that behave predictably and fail loudly.

Most of what I know came from shipping. At Marrymo I worked on a React Native matchmaking app across iOS and Android — onboarding, profile setup and match discovery — wired those screens to the backend, and took part in code reviews and production builds for the Play Store. Alongside that I've built a full MERN storefront with its own admin panel, an AI-powered interview practice app, and a Flask tool that scrapes job portals so I don't have to check them by hand.

Next up: going deeper on backend architecture and getting properly good at system design.
