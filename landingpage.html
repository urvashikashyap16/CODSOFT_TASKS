04:05 · before it gets light

Three steps, about four minutes

The whole point is that it should take less effort than staying awake with it.

Step 01

Tell us roughly what's going on

Three questions, all optional. Pick a topic or type your own words — one line is enough.

Step 02

We match you with a counsellor

Usually inside four minutes. You'll see their name, qualification and languages before the chat opens.

Step 03

Talk for as long as you need

No session timer. At the end you can save the chat, delete it, or book a follow-up for the same counsellor.

06:40 · morning

Is it really free?

Yes, for any student with a valid college ID. Nightlight is funded by partner institutions, so nothing is charged to you and nothing appears on a family bill.

Who am I actually talking to?

Qualified counsellors and clinical psychologists, each with a postgraduate qualification in psychology or counselling. You'll see their name and credentials before the chat starts.

Will my college find out?

No. Your chats aren't shared with your institution, and partner colleges only ever receive anonymous usage totals — never names, topics or transcripts.

What if it's an emergency?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 112. For urgent mental health support anywhere in India, Tele-MANAS is free and open around the clock on 14416.

The sun comes up either way

You just don't have to sit through the rest of the night on your own. Someone's online now.
